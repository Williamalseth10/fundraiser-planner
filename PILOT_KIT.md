# Pilot kit: real-quote sessions (drafts. Nothing has been sent.)

**Goal:** find out whether the planner changes a real decision (price, format, delay, verify, cancel). Whether people like it is not the measure.
**Who:** 3–5 high-school athletic booster organizers who are 4–12 weeks from a course decision and have, or can get, a written quote.

## Why a known club comes first
The quickest first session is with a club you already know, such as a team booster group at a school you have ties to. The risk is that friendly users are polite users. That's why the **decision record** below is completed *before* they see any output. A stranger-run session afterward checks whether the result holds up.

## Screening (5 questions, by phone or in person)
1. Is your club considering a golf outing in the next 3–6 months, or does it run one now?
2. Have you picked the format (tee times or shotgun) and the price yet?
3. Do you have a written course quote, or could you get one before we meet?
4. Who decides: you, a board vote, or the athletic director?
5. Could one person spend 45 minutes entering real numbers with us?
Proceed if the answers to 1 and 3 are yes and the decision is still open.

## Session script (45 min)
| Time | Step | Facilitator does / does not |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | **Decision record: before** (below) | Write their current plan down *before* opening the tool. Don't mention what the tool shows. |
| 8–30 | They click **Start blank plan** and enter the real quote, costs, sponsors and any evidence | Answer "where does this go?" questions. Don't suggest numbers. Note every hesitation or wrong field. |
| 30–38 | They read Step 3 aloud | Ask "What does this tell you?" Don't explain the status unless asked. Note misreadings. |
| 38–45 | **Decision record: after** + 3 questions | Record their own words. |

**After questions:**
1. Did anything here change what you'll do next? What exactly, and which number caused it?
2. What would have to be true for you to use this next year, or for the next event?
3. Would you pay anything for it, and in what form? (Ask only after 1 and 2.)

## Scoring (decided in advance)
- **Decision change** = a different value in the before/after table that the organizer attributes to a specific number in the tool. Saying "good to know" doesn't count.
- **Continue** if ≥ 2 organizers have a decision change.
- **Rethink** if 0 organizers have one. **Ambiguous** if 1: run 2 more sessions.
- Grok suggested also stopping if organizers "would not revisit" the tool. Since the event happens once a year, a low revisit rate is expected and isn't a stop signal by itself.

## Privacy and conduct
- Use the club's real numbers only on their device or with their permission. The file stays local, and they keep their own export.
- Collect no student information. Don't keep survey names; use counts only.
- Make no promises about results, tax status, or legal compliance.
- Contacting anyone requires William's approval. The recruitment message is Grok's draft and is unchanged.
