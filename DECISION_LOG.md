## Open review items
- Sponsorships are shared across options unless tied to one option. Confirm with a real club whether sponsors would follow a format change.
- Ask pilot organizers whether they already use GolfFund, Kismet, or a spreadsheet, and what this tool told them that those didn't.
- Product sales: the planner treats a supplier minimum or pre-order as the 'minimum billed'. Check with a real seller whether unsold-item returns need their own field.
- Pilot check (from audit): compare the first real course quote against the single venue formula; add a separate minimum only if the quote actually has one.
