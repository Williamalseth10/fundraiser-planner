# Start here: Fundraiser Planner

This planner helps you decide whether a fundraiser is worth committing to **before** anyone signs a contract or orders stock. You enter your real quote, costs, sponsors and supporter answers. It shows the cash you need first, what you'd lose if nobody comes or you cancel, how many sign-ups or sales you need, and what still needs checking.

It doesn't predict turnout, give tax or legal advice, or promise a result. "Not enough evidence to decide yet" is a normal, useful answer.

## Opening it
Use the [live website](https://williamalseth10.github.io/fundraiser-planner/), or double-click **Fundraiser Planner.html**. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox). You don't need the internet, an account or a password.

Tip: always open it on the **same computer, in the same browser**. That is where your plan is saved (see "Saving and backups" below).

## 1. Practice with the made-up example (about 5 minutes)
1. Choose **Try a golf example**. A yellow bar at the top reminds you that **every number in it is made up**.
2. Click **Next** through the steps to see what each one asks. You don't need to change anything.
3. Choose **See results**. Each option shows:
   - a **status** and what it means,
   - **Cash needed before event day**,
   - **Loss if nobody comes or you cancel** (if holding the event could lose even more, the card says so and gives the amount),
   - **Needed to break even**,
   - **Likely case** (your own estimate, not a prediction),
   - and a list called **Must verify before signing**.
4. Try one change: go back to **What will you charge?**, open the **Shotgun start** tab, change the price to **60** and the foursome price to **240**. Then look at the results again. That option now says **Not viable as currently configured**.

When you're ready for your own plan, choose **Start a new plan** on the home page. That replaces the example; you don't need to undo your practice changes.

## 2. Collect your real information
You can start with some of this missing. Anything missing shows up on the results page under **Must verify before signing**.

- [ ] **Written quote** from the course, venue or supplier: price per person or item, what it includes, tax and service charges, any minimum number or minimum bill, flat fees, and how many it can hold.
- [ ] **Deposit terms:** how much, when it's due, whether it counts toward the bill, and whether (and until when) it's refundable.
- [ ] **Cancellation terms:** what you'd owe if you cancel, and the deadline. Copy the dollar amount.
- [ ] **Your prices:** the single price and any group or table price you'd actually put on the form.
- [ ] **Other costs:** prizes, food not in the quote, printing, signs, insurance, supplies. Note which are paid before the event.
- [ ] **Sponsor commitments:** who has **paid** (collected), who has **promised in writing** (committed), and who you've only **asked** (hoped-for). Also donated items, and which cost each one replaces.
- [ ] **Actual receipts:** how many people have paid, and the total money actually received, from your records or bank statement.
- [ ] **Supporter survey totals:** how many answered, and for each option: "yes" with a name, anonymous "yes", and "maybe". Counts only, never names.
- [ ] **Volunteer hours:** how many the event needs, and how many your volunteers can really give.
- [ ] **Who signs:** whether the club, the school or the district signs and holds the money, and whether approval or insurance is required.

## 3. Enter your plan
Answer one step at a time. Each money box has a short explanation underneath it.

- Copy numbers from **written quotes and records**.
- If you're unsure of a number, leave it blank or enter your best guess, and leave **"I have this quote in writing"** unchecked. The planner treats it as unconfirmed.
- If you change a quote number later, the "in writing" box unchecks itself, so you remember to confirm the new number.
- Step 7 writes a short supporter survey with your exact prices. **Copy survey text**, then paste it into an email or Google Form for adult supporters.

## 4. Saving and backups (please read)
- Your plan **saves by itself**, but **only in this browser on this computer**.
- The website and downloaded HTML file keep separate saved plans. To move between them, use **Download backup file**, then **Open a backup file** in the other copy.
- It is **not** saved online. It will **not** appear on your phone or another computer.
- Clearing your browser history or site data, or using a private or incognito window, can **erase** it.
- To keep a safe copy: click **Save & backup** at the top, then **Download backup file**. A file named like `fundraiser-plan-2026-11-11.json` goes to your Downloads folder. Keep it somewhere safe, or email it to yourself.
- To open your plan later or on another computer: **Save & backup → Open a backup file**, then choose that file.
- If you load an example, start over, or open a file, your current plan is first kept as a backup in this browser. **Save & backup → Restore previous plan** brings it back.

Download a fresh backup file whenever you've made changes you'd hate to lose.

**If you see a red “Not saved” bar at the top:** your browser stopped saving (its storage may be full or blocked). Click **Download backup file** in that bar right away; the file includes your latest changes. The planner also refuses to replace your plan if it can't keep a backup first, and tells you so.

**Practice opening a file:** the file `practice-plan-fictional.json` (made-up numbers) came with this guide. Choose **Save & backup → Open a backup file** and pick it. A gala and a cookie dough sale appear, marked as made up. Then use **Restore previous plan** to go back to what you had.

## 5. Reading the results
| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Not enough evidence to decide yet** | Something important is missing: a written quote, real demand evidence, or enough paid or named "yes" answers to reach break-even. Collect it before signing. |
| **Not viable as currently configured** | Even if every spot sells, ticket money and firm sponsors don't cover the costs. Change the price, costs or format, or don't sign. |
| **High financial risk** | The plan can lose money under your numbers, or it only works if income you're hoping for (add-ons, auction money) actually arrives. |
| **Numbers hold up so far, but open items remain** | The numbers clear break-even with your evidence, but some things still need checking. Not a guarantee. |
| **Supported by current evidence (not a guarantee)** | The numbers clear break-even and nothing is flagged. It still doesn't guarantee a profit or make a contract safe. |

**Firm money** means ticket or sales money plus sponsorship that is paid or promised in writing. Break-even uses only firm money.

## 6. Sharing with the board
- **Copy board summary** copies a written summary you can paste into an email.
- **Print** prints the results, including every detail section. Printed examples stay marked as made up.
- The **What to tell the board (30 seconds)** box is a short script you can read aloud.

## What it won't do
- Guess how many people will come. You enter low, likely and high numbers yourself.
- Treat a survey "yes" as money.
- Give tax or legal advice, or promise a result.
- Send anything anywhere. Nothing leaves your computer unless you download or copy it yourself.
